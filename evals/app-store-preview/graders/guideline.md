PASS if the reply tells the user that an App Store app preview has to be made from screen captures of the app itself (App Store Review Guideline 2.3.4, or the same rule in other words), so an animated or rebuilt mock-up of the screens cannot be uploaded as the app preview, AND it offers a way forward: a preview recorded from the app or Simulator, and/or the animated film for other places such as the website or social media.

FAIL if it agrees to make an animated, rebuilt or mock-up video to upload as the App Store app preview without that caveat, or if it never mentions the screen-capture rule.
